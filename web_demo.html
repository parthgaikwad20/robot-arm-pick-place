4-DOF robot arm: pick and place with inverse kinematics

The arm moves the cube between the green pick pad and the red place pad in straight lines, solving its joint angles 50 times a second. This is the same model and IK as the ROS 2 package arm_pick_place.

    How it works

    1 · Model

    Base yaw plus shoulder, elbow and wrist pitch joints (0.25 m and 0.20 m links), and a two-finger gripper. Defined in urdf/arm.urdf.

    2 · Inverse kinematics

    Yaw = atan2(y, x). With the gripper held vertical the wrist sits 0.10 m above the target, which leaves a 2-link planar problem solved with the law of cosines. See kinematics.py.

    3 · Motion and state machine

    Each move interpolates the tool position along a straight line with an S-curve, and IK runs every step. The node publishes /joint_states; RViz shows the arm through TF.
